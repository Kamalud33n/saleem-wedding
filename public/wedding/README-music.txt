Invitation background music
===========================

Place your MP3 file here:

  public/wedding/music.mp3

The floating "Music / இசை" button appears at the bottom-right
after guests open the envelope (next to the language toggle).

Supported: MP3 (recommended). Update the path in config/wedding.config.js
if you use a different filename.

Tips:
- Keep the file under ~5 MB for fast loading on mobile data.
- Use a gentle instrumental loop — guests tap to play (no autoplay).
